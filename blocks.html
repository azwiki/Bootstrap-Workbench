How we build a piece

Every Northwind piece starts as rough boards that rest in the workshop for two weeks, so the wood settles to the room before we cut it.

Joints are cut by hand and glued with a water-based adhesive. We do not use metal fasteners where wood can hold the load.

Surfaces get three coats of hard wax oil. Each coat cures overnight and is buffed by hand the next morning.

Before a piece leaves, two people check it: one for the finish, one for stability on an uneven floor.

Delivery is by our own van within 80 km of the studio. Further away, a partner courier carries the piece in a padded crate.

If something arrives damaged, send a photo within seven days and we repair or replace it at no cost.

Spare felt pads, a tin of wax and a care card come with every order.

We keep the offcuts from your piece. Ask for them and we turn them into a matching set of coasters.
