What it is

Audit your theme

Load a demo page with your stylesheet and scroll. Unstyled buttons, bland alerts and off-brand subtle colors stand out right away.

Copy-paste snippets

Every example has a code toggle with clean, generated markup without demo wrappers, ready for your templates.

Bootstrap 5.3.8 in one place

Colors, utilities, components, forms and page blocks side by side, pinned to one version and checked against its stylesheet.

How it works

  1. Copy a page into your project

    Take classes.html, blocks.html or forms.html. Each one is a single file.

  2. Link your main.css

    The page already loads assets/css/main.css. Put your theme there or point the line at your stylesheet.

  3. Open it in a browser

    Double-click works: no server, no build step, no install.

  4. Spot the gaps

    Anything that still looks like stock Bootstrap is a class your theme does not cover yet.

The one line that matters

Keep it after the Bootstrap stylesheet so your theme wins.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="assets/css/main.css">

Who it's for

Theme authors

Before you ship a theme, check that outline buttons, subtle backgrounds, dark islands and form states all carry your palette, not the defaults.

WordPress child themes

Bootstrap-based parent themes such as picostrap compile your SCSS into one stylesheet. Point a demo page at it and see what your variables actually changed.

QA & design review

One page, every state. Review light and dark mode, three breakpoints and the token panel without clicking through a whole site.

Learning Bootstrap

Every example sits next to its markup. Toggle the code, copy it, change a class and watch what happens.
